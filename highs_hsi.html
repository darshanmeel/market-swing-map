Start from

Not a forecast. Each coloured line is one past fall that got as deep as the chosen start, followed for a year from the day it got there and applied to that start price. Click a name in the legend to hide it, double-click to show only that one. The "falls more" starts are drawn from today's date for easy reading; the real start is whenever that level is reached.

Daily guesses: what the most similar past falls did next

Every weekday after the close a new guess is saved: the past falls that got as deep as that day at the most similar speed, each replayed for a year from that day's close. Older guesses are kept, and each close after the guess day is added to its chart as the thick black line, so you can see which scenario it is following.
