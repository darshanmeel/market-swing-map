Return per year and the deepest close below the record, over the last 5 and the last 10 years.

Built by market_highs.py from Yahoo Finance daily closes. Returns are price only, without dividends; 5- and 10-year returns are per year. Volatility: the spread of daily moves over the last year, as a yearly figure; a normal day is that figure divided by the square root of 252. "Past 10%+ falls deeper": of the falls of 10% or more from a record in the last 10 years, how many went deeper than today. "Higher a year later": of the past falls that got as deep as today, how many were higher a year after they got there.
