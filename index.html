History, not a forecast or advice. These screens only sort markets by what their prices did; open a market for its swings, past falls and what came next. How this page works

Trend by group, at its reversal size

A turn counts only once the price moves back by the reversal size. A small size sees the recent swings, a big one the large moves, so one market can read Uptrend at 10% and Downtrend at 15%: both are right at their scale. Indices are read at 8%, commodities and crypto at 10%, companies at 10% and 15%.

Indices at 8% reversals: 5 and 10 years

Return per year and the deepest close below the record, over the last 5 and the last 10 years.

Built by market_highs.py from Yahoo Finance daily closes. Returns are price only, without dividends; 5- and 10-year returns are per year. Volatility: the spread of daily moves over the last year, as a yearly figure. "Past 10%+ falls deeper": of the falls of 10% or more from a record in the last 10 years, how many went deeper than today. "Higher a year later": of the past falls that got as deep as today, how many were higher a year after they got there.
